How I work

Purpose, principles, priorities. The three things behind every call I make.

PURPOSE

Give skilled people their time back.

Most of what I've shipped started with someone doing the same task by hand every week.

PRINCIPLES

Fail fast. Prototype first.

Build the cheapest version that can prove me wrong. Keep a person in the loop when a wrong answer costs trust.

PRIORITIES

Pain, risk, then speed.

How much manual pain, how often. What a wrong answer costs. How fast a first version can help someone.

Heads Up · concept · target under 1 second

Hear your name through the music.

With earbuds in, you miss the moment someone calls you. Heads Up listens for your name on the phone and breaks through.

Music playing. The phone keeps only the last 2 seconds of sound.
Someone calls “Chandra!” A cheap volume check notices the spike.
An on-device wake-word model finds your name in that buffer.
Music drops to 20% and a notification appears.
